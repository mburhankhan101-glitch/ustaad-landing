Most students get this one wrong.

The question

1024 can be written in scientific notation as

A1.024 × 10³
B2¹⁰
C0.000976
D1 / 0.00097

Most students pick B

1024 can be written in scientific notation as

A1.024 × 10³
✕2¹⁰
C0.000976
D1 / 0.00097

2¹⁰ = 1024. The maths is right.

Correct answer

1024 can be written in scientific notation as

✓1.024 × 10³
✕2¹⁰
C0.000976
D1 / 0.00097

But it is not scientific notation.
